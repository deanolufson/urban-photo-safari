Kansas City, circa 2000-2010

A photo contest, a meetup, and a four-hour excuse to see the city differently.

Urban Photo Safari began as a way for friends to learn their new digital cameras together. It grew into a roaming creative challenge with a map, a deadline, and a lot of very different ways of looking at the same streets.

How It Worked

Contestants met at Latte Land on the Country Club Plaza, signed in, received a downtown boundary map, and had four hours to shoot. Afterward, everyone turned in their photos, chose their ten favorites, and voted on the final gallery.

The Prize

The winner earned admiration, an Amazon gift certificate, and a T-shirt printed with the winning image. Everyone left with a CD of the day's photographs and a shared record of what happened when a group of creative people wandered the city with cameras.

This page is here as a small marker for Urban Photo Safari's past. Something new may happen here someday. For now, it remembers the pictures, the people, and the fun of the chase.
