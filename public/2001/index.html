Urban
Photo Safari.

Kansas City, Missouri May 05, 2001

At eight on a Saturday morning, nine photographers set out with digital cameras and four hours to make a picture. This was the first Safari.

From the first disc

“Not only were contestants shooting for the grand prize, they were shooting for each other.”Dean Olufson, introduction to the 2001 CD

Everyone took home a disc with the day’s photographs. The first ten in each gallery were the voting entries. Kevin Sisemore’s rain-soaked gas station took the prize. PurePLAY.tv and Netchemia sponsored the event.
