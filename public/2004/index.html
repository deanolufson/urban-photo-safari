➜ THE IDEA

Four hours. One city. Many ways to see it.

Friends with digital cameras met at Latte Land on the Country Club Plaza, received a downtown boundary map, and spent four hours photographing whatever caught their eye. Each person selected ten images to share and vote on. The results are on view here.
