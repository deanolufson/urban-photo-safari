A copy of this list was available the day of the contest. Signup started at 8 a.m. Saturday, May 22, 2004. Teams sharing one camera were allowed, with one prize per team.

Contest outline

  1. Meet at Latte Land on the Plaza (Nichols Rd location, not 47th St). Sign in and receive a map.
  2. Spend four hours shooting as many photographs as you want within the geographic boundary shown on the map.
  3. Return to Latte Land. Safari volunteers download all photographs.
  4. Choose your ten best and email the camera file names to Urban staff within one week.
  5. Urban staff post those ten photographs for voting. Each contestant has ten votes, cannot vote for their own photos, and may cast only one vote per photograph.
  6. Votes are tallied and a winner is announced on the site. Urban staff decide any ties.
  7. The winner receives a $25 Amazon.com gift certificate, an Urban T-shirt, and the admiration of others.

Requirements

  • Digital cameras only; no film cameras.
  • Minimum photo size: 640×480 pixels.
  • Take at least one self portrait. Every team member must appear in the team's self portrait.
  • Turn in every photograph taken during the contest at the end.
