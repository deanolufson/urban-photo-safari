Inspiration

In preparation for the upcoming Safari, we should post a few links to get us all pumped to shoot some amazing photos. Nothing like seeing what others are doing to get excited about getting out there and doing it yourself. There are the old standards such as National Geographic or Life. Image portfolios from some of the best photographers in the business.

But, every once in a while you find a site that screams “bookmark me!”. You keep going back to see the images over and over again. One of the things people have mentioned over the years is having some sort of image goal for Urban. For instance, say 2007 was kids. You’d have everyone go out and find kids to shoot. Excellent concept but, it works a little better to give people free range to shoot whatever they want. I mention this only to point out something you may want to try.

Take a look at some of your favorite photographers and see if you can recreate what they are doing. The style, the lighting, the subject matter. Whatever it is that you found inspiring, figure out how to do it with your own camera. Click over to the 2006 site and take a look at Eric Diebold’s photos. He spent the day trying to capture a washed out over lit image. Did a pretty good job. He also walked away from the Safari with one more lesson learned. If he ever needs to recreate that look again, he’ll know how.

So, with that in mind, you have any links? What sites or photographers inspire you? Post a comment for everyone to read and share your inspiration with the rest of us. Here’s a couple of jems I’ve collected over the years

  • overshadowed.com- Photographer Kieth Kin Yan
  • fotologue.jp - user submitted images
  • braingiants.com - photographer Charles Miller
