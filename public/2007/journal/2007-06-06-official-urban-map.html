Official Urban Map

With the help of the good folks at Indicia, the new 2007 map is out. Click the image to see a larger view. No need to print it out, this map will be included in the lanyard you receive during sign up. If you’ve never done the Safari, here’s the deal with the map…

In addition to the time limit of 4 hours, you may only shoot images within the boundaries of the map provided. It’s self policing so, yes, you could take an image in Topeka and say it was in the map. But, what would be the fun in that. This is the first year we’ve released the map ahead of time. Take advantage of it. Head over to Google Maps and find some interesting sites to see. Better yet, you could even scout locations ahead of time. I’m sure there’s also a few good Lawrence tourism sites. Know of any? Post a comment.
