you ready? part 2

Okay, so, a checklist. You think after doing this for 7 years I would have some sage advice on gettin’ ready. Main thing I could mention (which, Gwen so wisely mentioned in the comments of the last post) is to make sure your batteries are charged! Had that one happen to me more than once. DOH!, doesn’t even come close.

So, without further ado, here’s a pre-safari checklist:

Essential:

  1. Camera (seems obvious, but, I’ve actually forgotten mine once)
  2. Batteries
  3. Extra batteries
  4. Charge your batteries
  5. Good pair of walking/hiking shoes
  6. Gas up the auto, fill the tires in your bike. Whatever mode of transportation you choose, make sure it’s ready to roll.
  7. Couple friends (you told everybody you know right?)
  8. Bottled water
  9. Extra memory cards
  10. Directions to the event (click the link in the upper left corner of this page for a Google map.)
  11. Cell phone

Nice to have:

  1. Usb cable for your camera (we’ll have card readers on hand but, might come in handy if we don’t have your specific type of card reader)
  2. GPS device. Hey, I know, get a Garmin! (really shameless plug but, hey, they are pretty sweet)
  3. Backpack to stow camera gear, water, extra batteries etc.
  4. Extra lenses and lens filters (Urban is a great excuse to try new accessories for your camera. Don’t have any? Check your camera manufacturers website and see what’s available for your model)

These are the ones I could think of. You have any tips on what to bring? Up next: what to expect the day of…
