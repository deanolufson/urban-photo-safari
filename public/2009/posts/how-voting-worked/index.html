URBAN PHOTO SAFARI / 2009

How the 2009 vote worked

Each contestant chose ten photographs and a self portrait. The gallery kept photographers’ names hidden during voting.

Participants cast ten votes, with one vote allowed per photograph. The winner was determined by the final tally.
