URBAN PHOTO SAFARI / 2009

Thank you for coming out!

Thanks to everyone who came out to Homer’s on Saturday morning. We had four hours to wander Overland Park with our cameras, and by 1 p.m. everyone was back, loading pictures onto the laptops.

The next job was choosing ten photos and a self portrait for the gallery. That meant going through everything from the morning and deciding which ten pictures really belonged together.
