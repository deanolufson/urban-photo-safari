About the Safari

Urban Photo Safari began in 2001, when we needed to help a group of people get comfortable with new digital cameras. A morning outside seemed more fun than an office seminar. We gave everyone a map, a time limit, and a reason to look closely at the city.

By 2011, it had become an annual outing for friends, families, and photographers of all experience levels. We met at Mildred’s in the Crossroads on Saturday, June 18. Everyone had three hours to photograph within the boundaries, then returned at noon to have their pictures downloaded.

How the 2011 contest worked

  1. Bring a digital camera or camera phone and meet at Mildred’s to sign in.
  2. Photograph within the map boundaries from 9 a.m. to noon. Take at least eleven pictures, including one to represent yourself.
  3. Choose ten photographs and a self portrait from your shoot. Send the file names to Dean so they can be added to the gallery.
  4. Once the gallery is up, each contestant can vote for ten photographs by other contestants. Names stay hidden until voting ends.
  5. The photograph with the most votes wins. There were also optional Collision, Invade, and Illuminate theme votes.

There was no cash or gift prize in 2011. The pleasure was getting out with a camera, seeing what everyone found, and earning a little bragging rights.
