Giving Back

June 17th, 2011 by admin | Posted in Essentials

We have an event in Kansas City. Use it as our playground for a day. Use it as our canvas if you will. It would be a missed opportunity if we didn’t figure out a way to give back to what’s been given us. Each year, Urban has found various ways to give back to our host city with your help. This year, Urban is partnering with Hope Leadership Academy. Bring a few extra dollars you can donate to a great cause in our own city. Urban Photo Safari has always been a free event. Think of this as an entrance fee if you will. Your donation will  go a long way to helping a community in need of leadership and academic excellence. You may also donate online at: http://www.hlakc.org/donations.html. Help Urban be more than just taking photos but, about being a part of a community.
