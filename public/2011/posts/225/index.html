Email Address

June 20th, 2011 by admin | Posted in Post Event

One other minor note… take some time to look through the outline of the contest listed in the About section of this site. It’s a little copy heavy but, does outline what you need to know. Also, see the post below this one for information as well. There are some who are new to the contest who may be confused as to what the next steps are etc. One major question is “do I edit my photos and send them in? or, do I just send in the file names?”. The answer is… no editing. This is the reason for coming back to Mildred’s and having them downloaded. Urban is more about getting out and learning how to use your camera than a Photoshop contest. There are plenty of those out there so, Urban is a little different. Also, with close to 50 contestants, you can imagine how full my inbox would be not to mention how email can at times mess up photos. Any questions? Leave a comment.
