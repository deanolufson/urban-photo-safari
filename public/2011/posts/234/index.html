2011 Images Are Up!

June 23rd, 2011 by admin | Posted in Uncategorized

I’ve been at this for a while so, I’m taking the night off. To get you started here is what you need to do between now and Saturday afternoon. Please read carefully before you send in any questions. Gallery here: http://urbanphotosafari.com/2011

  1. Take a look at the gallery and most importantly of all, enjoy what everyone shot. Get ideas for your next shoot and get inspired. That is essentially what this is all about.
  2. You have 10 votes and cannot vote on your own.
  3. Write down your votes as you glance through the gallery. Should look something like: Contestant 01 – photo 02, Contestant 34 – photo 9 etc.
  4. Don’t forget about the Themed galleries at the end of page two of the gallery. These are additional votes. You must cast your first ten for contestants only and not the themed galleries. You have two additional votes for each gallery if you choose. You don’t have to cast for these, only if you want to. Again, like the other votes, you can’t vote on your own.
  5. You have until Saturday at noon to get me your votes. My email problems seem to have been solved so, send it to deanolufson (at) yahoo (dot) com. If you have problems, post a comment to this post.
  6. With all of the contestants, I have more than likely turned an image the wrong way or missed one. If so, shoot me an email and I can get it corrected a.s.a.p.
  7. Failure to get me your votes will exclude you from winning even if you do get the most votes. Tough task to look through so many but, realize there are other contestants more than likely voting for you. Take the time to go through and show our photo community the same respect they are showing you.

So, again, pick your 10 favorites. Write ‘em down and send them to me in an email to deanolufson (at) yahoo (dot) com. The contestant names are hidden until voting is over. Write down the cotestant # and the image # (contestant 22 – image 03 etc.). Have them to me by Saturday at noon. I tacked on an extra day for you to look through them considering it took me an extra day to put ‘em up.

If you don’t have your votes in, you’ll forfeit any votes you get and cannot win. So, grab a pad of paper, a cold beverage of your choice and browse through some great images. Everyone shot some pretty stunning stuff. Any questions? Shoot me an email.
