Mobile Version of the 2011 Gallery Available

June 24th, 2011 by admin | Posted in Uncategorized

If you haven’t seen it yet, this year’s gallery comes with a version compatible with Android, iPod, iPhone and iPad. All you have to do is visit the gallery page (http://urbanphotosafari.com/2011) and it will detect what OS you are using. If you haven’t tried it yet and have access to one of those devices, try it out. The navigation is slightly different than the gallery you see through a regular browser but, a pretty slick way to view the photos. scroll down a bit and look for the “view gallery” button. Let me know what you think in the comments.

Also, don’t forget the regular gallery can go full screen (upper right corner of gallery).
