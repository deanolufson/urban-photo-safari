[ THE PREMISE ]

Give a group of people the same streets, the same deadline, and the same brief.

What comes back is never the same.

Urban Photo Safari wasn’t really a photography contest.

It was a live experiment in perspective—a reason to move slower, look harder, and discover how differently creative people read the world.

[ RULES OF THE HUNT ]

Four hours.
No prescribed subject.
Follow your eye.

  1. 01MEET

    Latte Land, Country Club Plaza. Cameras ready. Coffee encouraged.

  2. 02ROAM

    A boundary map, a city, and exactly four hours to make something of it.

  3. 03EDIT

    Return with hundreds. Choose ten. Attention becomes authorship.

  4. 04SHARE

    Vote, trade stories, take home the whole day on a silver CD.

[ THE SHIFT ]

A contest became a community.
A website became a yearly design laboratory.

Every edition was a chance to rebuild the identity, rethink the interface, and test a new visual language in public.

That practice still matters: make the concept clear, create a system with range, and let every detail carry the same idea.

[ FIELD NOTES / 2026 ]

Still looking.
Still making.

UPS

Urban Photo Safari began as my creative playground. Reframed now, it connects two parts of my practice that were never truly separate: building durable product systems and creating expressive brand experiences.

The common thread is direction—finding the idea, making it legible, and giving teams a system they can carry forward.
